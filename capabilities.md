## Graceful Degradation

- **iCloud unavailable / signed out / simulator**: CloudKit service silently disables; SwiftData remains the source of truth, so map, post, dibs, chat, hauls and wanted flows all still work on a single device.
- **No AI backend** (old device without Apple Intelligence and no BYO key): `AIService` falls back to a deterministic heuristic that extracts a title, category and value band locally, and moderation falls back to a rule-based gatekeeper + human review queue.
- **Notifications denied**: in-app banners and badge counts replace system notifications; no feature blocked.

## No Configuration Needed

- Sign in with Apple (not used — local anonymous identity keeps first-launch friction at zero)
- Push notification service (local notifications only)
- HealthKit, Siri, App Groups, Watch (not used by this product)
- Background modes (all timers are in-app; no background refresh requirement)
